# Jason Knapp

I work on fractional product and AI leadership, helping teams turn product direction into shipped software and practical AI systems.

## Selected work

- [Cheapskate](https://github.com/jasonjknapp/cheapskate) routes AI tasks to models based on cost and a quality bar. Co-created with Bryan Knapp.
- [Knappstack](https://github.com/jasonjknapp/knappstack) documents a repeatable approach to building and reviewing software.
- [JJacked](https://github.com/jasonjknapp/jjacked-showcase) is an iPhone and Apple Watch workout app. The showcase includes architecture notes and selected Swift source.
- [AI Tool Atlas](https://jknapp.com/ai-tools/) is a browsable catalog of AI tools.
